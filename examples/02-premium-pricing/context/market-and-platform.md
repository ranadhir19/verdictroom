# Market and platform facts (public reference points)

- App store commission: 15% for small-business programme members,
  30% above the revenue threshold — every price is net of that cut.
- Consumer subscription apps most commonly price premium between
  $3 and $10 per month; $4.99 is a heavily used price point.
- Published benchmarks for free-to-paid conversion in consumer apps
  typically fall in the 2–5% range of monthly users; top-decile apps
  exceed it, most fall below.
- Annual plans generally show materially lower churn than monthly
  plans, but convert fewer users at the first paywall because the
  upfront commitment is larger.
- Add-on / modular pricing is common in prosumer tools but rare in
  casual consumer apps, where each additional purchase decision at a
  paywall measurably reduces completion.
